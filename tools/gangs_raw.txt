CHAOS RITUALS
The following Post-cycle Action are available to Chaos Helot cults and Chaos Corrupted gangs:
LEAD RITUAL (LEADER ONLY
Select which of the Chaos gods the ritual is attempting to invoke: the Blood God, the Plague Lord, the Dark Prince or the Architect of Fate (note: a Chaos Corrupted 
gang can only attempt to invoke the Chaos god they have dedicated themselves to).
Roll 2D6 and apply the following modifiers:
• +1 if the gang won a battle this Campaign Cycle.
• +1 if the gang gained Reputation this Campaign Cycle.
• +2 if the cult already has the favour of the god the ritual is invoking marked on their Gang Roster.
• - 2 if the cult has the favour of a different god to the one the ritual is invoking marked on their Gang Roster.
• - 1 if the gang lost a battle this Campaign Cycle.
• - 1 if the gang lost Reputation this Campaign Cycle.
If the final result is 9 or more, the ritual is heeded by the cult’s patron. Mark on their Gang Roster that they have the favour of their chosen god – the effects of this 
favour are listed as follows. If they already have the favour of a different god, it is replaced. In addition, if another Fighter perform the Ritual Focus Post-cycle Action, 
they immediately gain D3+3 XP.
If, however, the final result is a natural double 1, or is 2 or less after modifiers, the cult is deemed unworthy. They lose any favour they had with the gods. Furthermore,
if another Fighter performed the Ritual Focus Post-cycle Action, they are turned into a Chaos Spawn – remove the Fighter from the Gang Roster and add a Chaos 
Spawn to the Gang Roster.
RITUAL FOCUS (MAX ONE FIGHTER)
The Fighter offers themselves to be the focus of the Chaos Ritual. This may have additional effects on the Fighter as described in the Lead Ritual Post-cycle Action.
THE GOD’S FAVOUR
If the gang has successfully performed a Chaos Ritual and has marked on their Gang Roster the favour of their chosen god, the appropriate bonus is gained during the 
gang’s next battle:
The Blood God: Once per round, a single failed Wound roll can be re-rolled.
The Plague Lord: Once per round, a single Recovery roll can be re-rolled.
The Dark Prince: Once per round, during the Activation phase, two models that are Ready can be picked and activated one after the other, rather than the usual one. 
Neither of these models may perform the Group Activation action.
The Architect of Fate: Once per round, a single Hit roll can be re-rolled.

below, and treat these powers as one of their Primary Skill Sets.
1. Scouring (Free), Continuous Effect
For as long as this Wyrd Power is maintained, the Cult Witch counts as being armed
with the following weapon:
SR LR Str AP L Traits
Scouring T - 2 - 1 Blaze (5+), 
Template
2. Levitation (Free), Continuous Effect
For as long as this Wyrd Power is maintained, the Cult Witch’s Movement 
characteristic is increased by 3” and they gain the Flying Subtype.
3. Warp Strength (Free), Continuous Effect
For as long as this Wyrd Power is maintained, the Cult Witch’s Strength 
characteristic is increased by 2, and the Lethality of any close combat attacks they 
make is increased by 1.
4. Warp Shield (Single)
Until the End phase of this round, add 1 to the Save characteristic of the Cult 
Witch and any other friendly Fighters within 3” of them.
5. Maddening Visions (Single)
Until the End phase of this round, make a Willpower check for any enemy Fighter 
who ends their activation within 3” of the Cult Witch. If the check is failed, the 
Fighter gains the Insanity condition.
6. Assail (Single)
Immediately make a ranged attack against an enemy Fighter within 12” and Line 
of Sight. If Hit, move the target D3” in any direction and Suppress them. If they 
come into base contact with another Fighter or any terrain, they stop moving and
suffer a Str 3, AP -, L1 Hit. If they come into base contact with another Fighter, 
that Fighter also suffers a Str 3, AP -, L1 Hit and becomes Suppressed. If the two 
Fighters belong to different gangs, instead of both becoming Suppressed, they 
are now Engaged with each other.
CHAOS CORRUPTED GANGS
CHAOS CORRUPTED GANGS SPECIAL RULES
EMBRACING THE CHAOS GODS
During gang creation a player can decide that their gang has been corrupted by the Chaos gods. If they do, they gain the benefits and drawbacks that follow. The 
following gangs can be Chaos Corrupted: the six clan houses (Cawdor, Delaque, Escher, Orlock and Van Saar) and Palanite Enforcers.
EFFECTS OF CHAOS CORRUPTION
A Chaos Corrupted Gang gains the following benefits and drawbacks:
• Members of the gang do not benefit from any of the gang’s special rules except for Starting Skills and Skill Access.
• The gang must select one of the Chaos gods to dedicate themselves to.
• The gang may perform both the Lead Ritual and Ritual Focus Post-cycle Actions
• Chaos Helot Cult Witches may be added to the Gang Roster
• 0-1 Chaos Spawn may be added to the Gang Roster.
• Leaders and Champions may purchase Chaos Familiars.
• No Fighters with the Brute, Hanger-on or Pet Subtype may be purchased from the gang’s list.
Dedicated to a Chaos God: When the gang is founded, it must select one of the Chaos gods to dedicate themselves to: the Blood God, the Plague Lord, the Dark Prince
or the Architect of Fate.
May Perform Lead Ritual & Ritual Focus Post-cycle Actions: The gang’s Fighters may perform both the Lead Ritual and Ritual Focus Post-cycle Actions. When the 
gang’s Leader performs the Lead Ritual Post-cycle Action, they can only attempt to invoke the blessing of the Chaos god the gang is dedicated to.

GENESTEALER CULT WYRD POWERS
Genestealer Cult Adepts and any Genestealer Corrupted gang Leader upgraded to have the Wyrd Subtype have access to the Genestealer Cult Wyrd Powers listed 
below, and treat these Powers as one of their Primary Skill Sets.
1. Hypnosis (Single)
Select an enemy Fighter that is both within 9” and Line of Sight of the Adept, and 
that has a Ready marker. That Fighter must end their next activation closer to the 
Adept than they were at the start of their activation. If this is not possible the 
Fighter suffers a Str -, AP -, L1 Hit that Wounds automatically and ignores all saves.
2. Unbreakable Will (Free), Continuous Effect
Whenever a Cool or Willpower check is made for a friendly Fighter within 9” of the 
Adept, use the Adept’s Cool or Willpower characteristic instead of the Fighter’s 
(whichever is appropriate).
3. Zealot (Single), Continuous Effect
All friendly Fighters within 9” of the Adept gain the Hatred (Everything) Condition.
4. Mind Control (Single)
Choose a single enemy Fighter that is both within 9” and Line of Sight of this 
Fighter. That Fighter immediately makes a Shoot action, even if they have already 
activated this round, targeting an eligible Fighter from their gang, chosen by this 
Fighter’s controlling player.
5. Assail (Single)
Immediately make a ranged attack against an enemy Fighter within 12” and Line 
of Sight. If Hit, move the target D3” in any direction and Suppress them. If this 
movement would cause a Fighter to fall, they stop at the edge of the platform 
and test to see if they fall. If they come into base contact with another Fighter or 
any terrain, they stop moving and suffer a Str 3, Ap -, L1 Hit. If they come into 
base contact with another Fighter, that Fighter also suffers a Str 3, AP, L1 Hit and 
becomes Suppressed. If the two Fighters belong to different gangs, instead of 
both of them becoming Suppressed, they are now Engaged with each other.
6. Force Wave (Single)
Any enemy Fighters within 3” of the Adept are immediately pushed D3+1” 
directly away. If this movement would cause a Fighter to fall, they stop at the 
edge of the platform and test to see if they fall. If they come into base contact 
with another Fighter or any terrain, they stop moving and suffer a Str 3, Ap -, L1 
Hit. If they come into base contact with another Fighter, that Fighter also suffers a
Str 3, Ap -, L1 Hit and becomes Suppressed. If the two Fighters belong to different
gangs, instead of both of them becoming Suppressed, they are not Engaged with 
each other.

GENESTEALER INFESTATION
During gang creation a player can decide that their gang has been corrupted by a Genestealer Cult. If they do, they gain the benefits and drawbacks that follow. The 
following gangs can become a Genestealer Cult Corrupted Gang: the six clan houses (Cawdor, Delaque, Escher, Orlock and Van Saar) and Palanite Enforcers.

A Genestealer Cult Corrupted Gang gains the following benefits and drawbacks:
• Members of the gang do not benefit from any of the gang’s special rules except 
for Starting Skills and Skill Access.

BOMB DELIVERY RATS
30 credits – Exclusive
A Fighter equipped with bomb delivery rats may perform the Prime Bomb Rat 
action.
Prime Bomb Rat (Single): Select a grenade with the Blast (3”/5”) trait the Fighter is
equipped with that is not Out of Ammo and make an Ammo (X+) test for them as if
the grenade had been fired. Then, place a bomb delivery rat in base contact with 
this Fighter which then immediately activates. At the start of each round, after 
rolling for Priority but before the first model activates, all bomb delivery rats 
activate. When a bomb delivery rat activates, follow these instructions:
• If they are within 9” of the Fighter who placed them, that Fighter’s controlling 
player may choose which direction they move in, otherwise they move in a 
direction dictated by the Scatter dice.
• When they activate they move 6” in a straight line, are not slowed by terrain, 
have the Clamber skill, can never fall or be pinned, and automatically pass Agility 
tests to jump gaps of up to 2”.
• If they come up to a gap larger than 2” or impassable terrain, they will stop.
• If they move within 1” of any model (other than the Fighter who placed them), 
they stop and may explode. Roll a D6. On a 2+, the grenade explodes; centre it on 
the bomb delivery rat. On a roll of a 1, the grenade fails to explode and the bomb 
delivery rat escapes. Regardless of the result, remove the bomb delivery rat from 
the battlefield.
• The bomb delivery rat may be targeted by close combat and ranged attacks. All 
attempts to hit them suffer a -1 modifier to the Hit roll. If the rat is Hit, roll a D6; on
a 4+, the grenade explodes (see above), otherwise the bomb delivery rat escapes. 
Either way, remove them from the battlefield.

The following are Wyrd Powers are available to Delaque Fighters. Any Delaque Fighter with the Wyrd Subtype may select Psychoteric Whispers as an Advancement as

failed, they gain the Insanity Condition.
2. Psychoteric Lure (Single): Choose a single enemy Fighter that currently has a 
Ready marker and is within 6” and Line of Sight of the Wyrd. The opposing player 
must activate that Fighter next. The chosen Fighter cannot perform the Group 
Activation action when next activated.
3. Deceiful Thoughts (Double): Choose a single enemy Fighter that is within 6” and 
Line of Sight of the Wyrd. That Fighter immediately makes a Willpower check. If 
this check is failed, the Wyrd’s controlling player may immediately move that 
Fighter up to their Movement characteristic in any direction (note: this move may 
not be used to move enemy Fighters off ledges, into dangerous terrain or any other
part of the battlefield harmful to them).
4. Cacophony of Silence (Single), Continuous Effect: While this power is 
maintained, all enemy Fighters within 6” and Line of Sight of the Wyrd must re-
roll successful Hit rolls when making a ranged attack.
5. A Perfect Void (Free), Continuous Effect: While this power is maintained, the 
Wyrd counts as always being in cover and within a weapon’s Long Range to all 
enemy Fighters within 10”.
6. Eternal Slumber (Single): Choose a Seriously Injured enemy Fighter within 3” 
and Line of Sight of the Wyrd, they immediately go Out of Action.

MALSTRAIN CORRUPTED SPECIAL RULES
WILL OF THE PATRIARCH
During gang creation, a player can decide that their gang has been corrupted by the Malstrain taint. If they do, they gain the benefits and drawbacks that follow. The 
following gangs can be Malstrain corrupted: the six Clan Houses (Cawdor, Delaque, Escher, Goliath, Orlock and Van Saar) and Palanite Enforcers.
EFFECTS OF MALSTRAIN CORRUPTION
A Malstrain corrupted gang gains the following benefits and drawbacks:
• Members of the gang do not benefit from any of the gang’s special rules except 
for starting skills and skill access.
• The gang leader may be upgraded to be a Wyrd.
• 0-3 Malstrain Genestealers and 0-1 Malstrain Coalescence may be added to the 
Gang Roster.
• Leaders and Champions may purchase Malstrain Tyramites.
• Any number of Malstrain Brood Scum may be added to the Gang Roster.
• No Fighters with the Brute, Hanger-on or Pet Subtype may be purchased from the
gang’s list.
Upgrading the Leader to a Wyrd: the Leader of a Malstrain corrupted gang can be 
upgraded to become a Wyrd for +35 credits. This cost is either paid during the Post-
cycle Sequence and requires the Leader to forgo any Post-cycle Actions and to not 
be In Recovery, or it may be selected when they are recruited. Once the Leader has
been upgraded, they gain the Wyrd Subtype and can choose one Wyrd power from 
the Malstrain Wyrd Powers list. Thereafter, they count Malstrain Wyrd Powers as 
one of their Primary Skill Sets.
Hiring Malstrain Genestealers & Malstrain Coalescence: Malstrain corrupted 
gangs can include up to three Malstrain Genestealers and up to one Malstrain 
Coalescence in their gang.
Malstrain Tyramites: Leaders and Champions in Malstrain corrupted gangs can 
be equipped with up to two Malstrain Tyramites each.
Hiring Malstrain Brood Scum: Malstrain corrupted gangs may add Malstrain 
Brood Scum to their gang roster as if they were part of their gang list.
No Brutes, Hangers-on or Pets From Core Equipment List: Cutting ties with their 
original gang, Malstrain corrupted gangs may not add Fighters with the Brute or 
Hanger-on Subtypes from their gang list to their Gang Roster, nor may they 
purchase any pets from their equipment list.

DESIGNER'S NOTE: ELEVATING A FIGHTER TO LEADER STATUS
There are many kinds of Outcast Leaders that can be created using the profiles, Archetypes and Affiliations presented in this section. It might be, however, that you 
want to create an Outcast gang centred around an existing fighter from another gang list or Necromunda publication. This is entirely appropriate, as any Fighter 
might rise up to lead a gang at some point or another in their careers.
• The first step is to choose a Fighter to become your Outcast Leader. This can be any Fighter with a profile, whether they could normally be part of a gang or not. 
Dramatis Personae, Hangers-on and Hired Guns are particularly suited to becoming gang Leaders and should prove easy to convert. Other kinds of Fighters might 
need more work, and some should probably only be allowed with the agreement of the campaign Arbitrator (as fun as it would be to have a gang of Outcasts led by 
an intelligent Sumpkroc).
• The Fighter retains any skills, equipment or special rules they might have. Alternatively, you can replace their skills and equipment with those available to an 
Outcast Leader. Whichever option is taken, you need to choose an appropriate Affiliation and Archetype for this Fighter.
• The Fighter retains their cost in credits, and throughout a campaign gains Experience and can be equipped with wargear like a normal Fighter.
• The Fighter gains all the special rules an Outcast Leader has.
Note that, due to the incredibly wide variety of Fighters available to be elevated to an Outcast Leader, the campaign Arbitrator always has the final say on whether 
these rules can be used and how they are applied.
ARCHETYPE
Every Outcast Leader has different strengths and weaknesses. Some are brutal overlords who rule through physical strength, crushing all in their path; others are more 
cunning, manipulating those around them to do their bidding; others still call upon the powers of the Warp to keep their gang in line. Archetypes allow you to further 
customize your Outcast Leader by giving them access to different Skill Sets.
There are five different Archetypes: Brawler, Gunslinger, Mastermind, Survivor, and Wyrd. When an Outcast Leader is recruited for an Outcast gang they must choose 
one of these Archetypes, and it may not be changed later on. This Archetype will also determine the skills available to all Outcast models except Champions (note: 
Champions may choose a different Archetype to their gang's Leader).

AFFILIATION
To represent the huge variety of potential Outcast Leaders, when an Outcast gang is created its Leader must choose an Affiliation. An Affiliation is a glimpse into the 
origins of the Leader, or speaks to the contacts they still maintain with those more mainstream members of hive society. There are four different Affiliations an Outcast
Leader can choose from: Clanless, Clan House, Aranthian or Mutant. Each comes with its own advantages and disadvantages, but most importantly will determine the 
equipment that the Outcast Leader and their gang has access to. Once the choice of Affiliation has been made, it cannot be changed – not even an Outcast can escape 
their past.
CLANLESS OUTCAST GANGS
A Clanless Outcast Leader and their gang gain the following benefits:
• Clanless Outcast gangs gain additional TPs as covered below:
   - At gang creation, each Leader and Champion in a Clanless Outcast gang gains an 
additional 1 TP that may be spent on weapons and wargear from the Trading Post.
   - During each Post-cycle Sequence, the first Visit Trading Post Post-cycle Action 
performed by a Clanless Outcast gang generates an additional 1 TP.
CLAN HOUSE OUTCAST GANGS
A Clan House Outcast Leader and their gang gain the following benefits:
• When a Clan House Outcast gang is created, you must choose one of the six Clan 
Houses for them to affiliate with: Cawdor, Delaque, Escher, Goliath, Van Saar or 
Orlock. For scenario or campaign purposes, the Outcast gang is considered to be a 
Clan House gang of the chosen type.
• Leaders and Champions in a Clan House Outcast gang may purchase weapons 
and wargear normally restricted to their house equipment lists, including Pets, at 
gang creation and during each Post-cycle Sequence.
MUTANT OUTCAST GANGS
A Mutant Outcast Leader and their gang gain the following benefits:

the Aranthian equipment list at gang creation and during each Post-cycle

NEW SUBTYPE: SPYRER
There are a number of special rules that apply to a Spyrer:
Kill Count: Each Spyrer hunting rig records all that happens to the Spyrer, verifying 
their kills and unlocking functions of the hunting rig and its weaponry as their Kill 
Count ticks up, making them an ever more dangerous foe. In addition to gaining XP,
each time a Spyrer takes an enemy Fighter Out of Action, increase the Spyrer’s Kill 
Count by 1.
Threat Assessment: When selecting starting Crews or Reinforcement, divide the 
number of models allowed by two, rounding up, to determine the number of 
Spyrers that may be selected. For example, if 3 Reinforcements would arrive, only 
two Spyrers arrive.
Master of the Hunt: All Spyrers have a suite of special rules granted to them by 
their hunting rigs:
• All Spyrers have the Juggernaut skill.
• A Spyrer may never be Captured for any reason; rather, they are considered to 
have suffered Superficial Damage.
• Spyrers do not suffer side effects from chems and can never become addicted 
to them.
• A Spyrer never needs to make Nerve check.
Reflex Enhancers: During the Ready Gang step of the Priority Phase, all Spyrers 
gain one extra Ready marker; if an effect would prevent them from gaining a 
Ready marker, they gain one fewer Ready marker. Each time a Spyrer activates, 
they only remove one Ready marker – this means they will be able to activate 
twice each round. Any effect that removes Ready markers or prevents them from 
being added only affects one Ready marker.

Spyrers do not have access to the standard Post-cycle Actions; instead, they may only perform the following Post-cycle Actions:
SUIT EVOLUTION (SPYRER ONLY)
If a Spyrer’s Kill Count is at least 4, reduce their Kill Count by 4, and either clear all 
Spyrer Hunting Rig Glitches (resetting their Glitch Count to zero and removing all 
negative effects caused by the Glitches), or gain a Hunting Rig Augmentation as 
follows. If the Spyrer’s Kill Count is still at least 4, they may perform this action 
again for free.

its systems and brings additional functions online. Pick one weapon or piece of 
wargear carried by the Spyrer and increased its Augmentation tier by 1. If the new 
Augmentation would increase a characteristic beyond its maximum increase the 
Augmentation tier by an additional 1.
SUIT MAINTENANCE (SPYRER ONLY)
The Fighter repairs and maintains their hunting rig. The gang can remove 100 
credits from their Stash in order to clear all Spyrer Hunting Rig Glitches (resetting 
their Glitch Count to zero and removing all negative effects cause by the glitches).
TERRORISE TERRITORY (SPYRER ONLY – MAX FIVE FIGHTERS)
The Spyrer goes out into a Territory, subduing the local populace and flushing out 
any Aranthian sympathizers. Add 10 credits to the gang’s Stash for each Spyrer 
that performs this action.
TRAIN (SPYRER ONLY)
The Spyrer practises their skills ready for the upcoming battles. Each Spyrer that 
performs this action earns 2 XP.
