Первые принципыFirst Principles

Базовые соглашения: кто такие модели, как измеряются расстояния, когда можно передумать.

In any game, there are certain conventions for all players to be aware of. In Necromunda, these include such things as how to roll and read dice, how distances are measured, when it is acceptable to change one's mind, and so forth.

Бойцы, машины и моделиFighters, Vehicles & Models

  • Any model that represents a single humanoid or a single animal is referred to as a Fighter and has the Fighter Type. This could be a lowly gang member, a renowned hired gun, or even an exotic beast owned by a mighty gang Champion.
  • Any model that is Mounted on an animal or bike is a Fighter with the Mounted Subtype.
  • Any model that represents a Vehicle of any type is referred to as a Vehicle and has the Vehicle Type.
  • Where the rules use the terms Fighter or Vehicle they refer specifically to models of that Type. Where the rules use the term model, they refer to both Fighters and Vehicles.

Controlling & Opposing Player

Frequently, rules will refer to the controlling player — this is the player whose model is being affected; the opposing player is their opponent.

Take-backs & Changing One's Mind

As a general rule, players should be tolerant of an opponent second-guessing themselves. However, once dice have been rolled for any reason, players must abide by their decision; they can no longer go back and change anything that came before the dice roll.

Измерение расстоянийMeasuring Distances

All distances are measured in inches (") with a range ruler or tape measure. Distances can be measured at any time by either player.

Distances between models and any other battlefield objects (terrain features, objectives, and so forth) are measured from the closest point of one base to the closest point of the other. If a model or an object does not have a base, measure to or from the closest point of the model or object overall.

Unless stated, distances cannot be measured through any terrain feature defined as being solid. If two models are at different elevations, or levels, measure the distance directly from one base to the other.

Arbitrator's Toolkit: No Pre-Measuring If an Arbitrator wishes, they may allow players to measure distances only after an action has been declared. If the action is not possible (due to a target being outside a weapon's Long Range) the model still attempts it, and would still make a shot and so potentially suffer the effects of the Unstable, Ammo (X+) and similar traits.

Линия видимости и укрытиеLine of Sight & Cover

Что боец видит и насколько цель прикрыта — определяется взглядом «от модели».

Секторы обзораFighter Vision Arcs

Fighters are assumed to be constantly looking around them, keeping a wary eye, ear, and nose out for danger. As such, all models have a 360° vision arc.

The Arc (X) trait: a weapon with this trait may only target enemy models in the model's arcs denoted by X. If the arc is Front, Rear, Left or Right, determine the arcs by drawing two imaginary lines through the corners of the model. If the arc is Centreline, determine the arc by drawing two imaginary lines along the side of the model.

Линия видимостиLine of Sight

To check one model's Line of Sight to another model, stoop down to look from the first model's point of view.

  • If a straight, uninterrupted line can be drawn from the first model to any part of the other model, the first model has a Line of Sight to them.
  • If no such line can be drawn due to intervening terrain or the presence of other models, the first model does not have a Line of Sight to them.

Exclude their base and any insignificant elements, such as a protruding hairstyle, the barrel of a gun, or a spike from their armour. A Line of Sight can never be drawn through any terrain feature defined as being solid.

Agreeing on Line of Sight It pays to be gracious when your opponent claims Line of Sight. If both players take this approach, the game will flow more smoothly. If players cannot agree, it should be settled by rolling off.

УкрытиеCover

To see if a target is in cover, stoop down to look from the attacker's point of view in exactly the same manner as described for checking Line of Sight. However, when checking to see if the target is in cover, disregard any friendly Fighters in base contact with the attacker — moving them aside temporarily if necessary, as long as they return to the original place once the shot has been resolved.

  • If the target is fully visible, they are not in cover (i.e., they are in the open).
  • If any of the target is obscured by terrain or models, they are in cover.

The benefits of cover are applied as modifiers to the Save roll (см. Разрешение попаданий).

МестностьTerrain

Типы ландшафта, которые согласуются до начала боя.

Impassable Terrain
Any terrain can be designated impassable when setting up the battlefield, regardless of size. Impassable terrain may include solid sections of wall, vertiginous rock formations or towering vats of molten metal. It cannot be moved through or over.
Difficult or Dangerous Terrain
Ranges from acidic pools of sludge to debris strewn floors. Any terrain can be designated as difficult or dangerous when setting up the battlefield, regardless of size. It is difficult to move over and can even be deadly to those traversing it.
Walls
Walls are almost always considered impassable terrain. On a battlefield of only one level, it is assumed that at the top of each wall sits a ceiling preventing Fighters from climbing over. On a multi-level battlefield, featuring ladders, staircases and even lifts, it is possible for Fighters to scale and climb over walls.
Doors
Set up as part of the Pre-battle Sequence. By default, all doors are closed at the start of a battle. Closed doors are considered impassable terrain and block both Line of Sight and measurement. Any Fighter may open a closed door or close an open door within 1" of them by sacrificing 2" of their movement during their activation. Closed doors can be targeted by attacks and are automatically hit; all doors have a Toughness of 5, and if a door is Wounded four times it is destroyed (do not roll Injury dice for doors).
Obstacles
Any freestanding terrain features measuring no more than 2" high and no more than 2" across, such as barricades, barrels and pipelines.
Structures
Any terrain features measuring more than 2" high or more than 2" across, be they freestanding or connected to other terrain features. Structures may feature many different levels, platforms and walkways on which models may be placed. Vehicles are unable to climb over obstacles or up onto structures.
Agreeing on Terrain Types After players have finished setting up the battlefield, it is worth going through each piece of terrain and agreeing what they all count as, to avoid confusion during the battle.

Кубики и броскиNecromunda Dice & Rolling Dice

Типы кубиков и терминология бросков: тесты, проверки, модификаторы, переброс.

Типы кубиковNecromunda Dice

D6
A regular six-sided dice, marked 1 to 6. On many Necromunda dice it is common for the '6' to be replaced by a logo.
D66
Roll two D6, one after the other, counting the first dice as tens and the second as units, giving a result between 11 and 66. For example, a roll of 2 then 4 gives 24.
D3
Roll a D6 and halve the result, rounding fractions up.
Firepower dice
Used when a model makes a ranged attack with a weapon that has the Rapid Fire (X) trait, to determine how many shots the weapon makes and whether it runs Out of Ammo. Six-sided: three bullet symbols on one face, two bullets on two faces, one bullet on two faces, and a bullet plus an ammo symbol on one face.
Scatter dice
Six-sided, marked with a Hit symbol (a target icon with a small arrow) on two faces and an arrow on each of the other four. Used to determine random directions, most often for weapons with the Blast (3"/5") trait.
Injury dice
Determines the severity of an Injury: an Injured symbol on two faces, a Seriously Injured symbol on three faces, and an Out of Action symbol on one face.

Броски, тесты и проверкиRolling Dice

Single dice rolls
'Roll a D6'. Sometimes several single dice are rolled at once — for example, 3D6 means three dice are rolled together, but the result of each dice is considered separately.
Multiple dice rolls
Roll multiple D6 and add the results together, e.g. 2D6 gives a total between 2 and 12.
Dice pools
Two or more dice are rolled and a single result selected — for example, rolling two or three Injury dice and selecting one result to apply.
Tests
A player needs to roll a specific number or higher, shown as a number followed by '+'. If a 4+ is required, a roll of 4, 5 or 6 succeeds.
Checks
A player needs to roll equal to or under a specific number, shown as a plain number. If a check against 3 is required, a roll of 1, 2 or 3 succeeds.
Modifying Dice Rolls If a +1 modifier is applied to a test, add it to the dice result; if it is applied to a check, add it to the target number. Positive modifiers make it more likely for the test or check to pass, negative modifiers less likely. If both positive and negative modifiers apply, apply all positive modifiers first, then all negative modifiers. Any fractions are rounded up unless stated otherwise.
Rolling off
Both players roll a dice (usually a D6), occasionally adding a characteristic. The highest score always wins a roll-off. In the case of a tie, roll again unless otherwise instructed.
Natural rolls
The actual number rolled on a dice, regardless of modifiers (including the halving of the result when rolling a D6 as a D3).
Re-rolls
The second result must always be accepted, even if it is worse than the original. A dice can never be re-rolled more than once, regardless of the source of the re-roll.

Тесты и проверки характеристикCharacteristic Tests / Checks

  • Weapon Skill and Ballistic Skill tests are made by achieving a target number on a single D6. If the result is equal to or higher than the characteristic, the test is passed.
  • Leadership, Cool, Willpower and Intelligence checks are made by rolling equal to or under the characteristic on 2D6.
  • Any other characteristic presented as a simple number (such as Strength or Toughness) is checked by rolling a D6 equal to or lower than the characteristic.
Improving / Reducing Characteristics When the characteristic is a simple number, it is just increased or decreased by the appropriate amount. If the characteristic is given as a target number, it is improved by lowering the number and reduced by increasing it — a model with Ballistic Skill 4+ that improves their BS by +1 now has BS 3+.

Short Range (SR) & Long Range (LR)
Weapons cannot Hit models outside of their Long Range. A range of 'E' means the weapon can only be used in close combat against targets Engaged with the wielder. A range of 'T' means it uses the flame template. A range of '–' means the weapon cannot be used at that range.
Strength (Str)
Used when making Wound rolls. For close combat weapons this may be shown as 'S' (use the wielder's Strength) or 'S' with a modifier such as S+3. If a weapon's Strength is '–', its traits describe how to resolve hits from it.
Armour Piercing (AP)
A modifier applied to the dice rolled to make an Armour save. Most often negative, but it may be positive or '–' (no modifier).
Lethality (L)
How many Injury dice are rolled against a target that has been reduced to zero Wounds as a result of the attack.
Traits
Most weapons have one or more traits, each of which gives the weapon a unique bonus or changes the way in which it works.
Credit Cost (Creds) / Trade Points (TP)
The cost to purchase the weapon from the Trading Post, and how easily available it is there.

Статусы моделейModel Status

Статус определяет, какие действия доступны модели при активации.

Active
A model is Active if they are not currently Engaged with any enemy models, Suppressed or Seriously Injured/Damaged. Active is the default Status and such models enjoy the greatest freedom to perform actions.
Engaged
In order for models to make close combat attacks, they must be Engaged with an enemy model:
  • If the base of a Fighter is touching the base of an enemy Fighter, they are in base to base contact and are Engaged, unless either Fighter is Seriously Injured/Damaged.
  • If the base of a Fighter is within 1" of any part of an enemy Vehicle at the start of their activation, they may choose to be Engaged with that Vehicle.
  • If any part of a Vehicle is within 1" of any part of an enemy model at the start of its activation, it may choose to be Engaged with that model.
An Engaged model can generally only choose actions that allow them to fight or retreat.
Suppressed
Models generally become Suppressed as a result of being hit by enemy fire, and may perform one less action during their activation. If a Suppressed model ever becomes Engaged, they immediately change their Status to Engaged — a model can never be both. The Suppressed marker is removed at the end of their next activation.
Seriously Injured / Damaged
If a model suffers a 'Serious Injury' result on an Injury dice, they become Seriously Injured (Fighter) or Seriously Damaged (Vehicle). Such a model is very vulnerable, susceptible to enemy attacks against which they cannot defend themselves and unable to move quickly towards safety. If they regain a Wound, change their Status to Active; if this happens while they are within 1" of an enemy model, they are instead taken Out of Action.
Multiple Statuses A model may only ever have one Status at a time. When multiple Statuses would apply, use this priority order:
  1. Seriously Injured/Damaged
  2. Engaged
  3. Suppressed
  4. Active

МаркерыShowing Conditions & Statuses

The following types of markers can be placed next to models to indicate their Status or Condition: Ready & Activated, Suppressed, Seriously Injured/Damaged, One Wound & Two Wound, Injured, Insanity, Webbed, Out of Ammo.

Подтипы бойцовFighter Subtypes

Подтип определяет место бойца в иерархии банды и доступные ему пост-цикловые действия. У бойца может быть несколько подтипов одновременно.

Beast
An animal that can only perform limited actions. Unless stated otherwise, a Beast may not perform Interact or Treat Ally. Additionally, a Beast may not assist a model making a Recovery test, and a gang cannot use the Leadership characteristic of a Beast for Bottle checks unless every friendly model on the battlefield is a Beast.
Brute
All Brutes have the Juggernaut skill. When a Brute is removed from the Gang Roster, all weapons and wargear they are equipped with are deleted instead of going into the gang's Stash.
Champion
Friendly models within 6" and in Line of Sight of a Champion may use the Champion's Cool characteristic for Nerve checks. All Champions have the Inspiring skill.
Flying
May move between levels without needing to climb a wall or ladder, by measuring a straight line between their starting and final positions. Whenever they fall, they are not Suppressed and never take any damage. They may not ignore impassable terrain and may not end their movement overlapping an obstacle or another Fighter's base.
Ganger
If during the Post-cycle Sequence a Ganger reaches at least 37 XP, they are promoted to a Champion. After rolling to determine an Advancement, they replace the Ganger Subtype with the Champion Subtype, keeping all their other existing Subtypes.
Hanger-on
May be excluded from being randomly selected when choosing a starting crew or creating a Reinforcements deck. When removed from the Gang Roster, all their weapons and wargear are deleted instead of going into the gang's Stash.
Leader
One Fighter in every gang must always have the Leader Subtype; if they die, another Fighter is promoted to replace them. Friendly models within 12" and in Line of Sight of a Leader may use the Leader's Cool characteristic for Nerve checks. All Leaders have the Inspiring skill.
Loner
A gang cannot use the Leadership characteristic of a Loner for Bottle checks unless every friendly model on the battlefield is either a Beast or a Loner.
Mounted
  • Optional Equipment: the controlling player decides before determining starting crews whether a Fighter will use mount wargear; this cannot be changed mid-battle.
  • Skills: all Mounted Fighters gain the Nerves of Steel and Hit & Run skills.
  • Terrain: Mounted Fighters cannot climb up nor down walls or ladders, but can still jump down to lower levels or jump gaps.
  • Gun It: when performing a Charge or Dash action, they may only make one 90° turn.
  • Hands Full: cannot be equipped with any weapon that has the Heavy or Paired trait unless it also has the Lance or Lance-bomb trait, and may not use a Secondary weapon in close combat.
Pet
Purchased as wargear for a model; their credits value is included on the owning model's Model Card. Automatically included in a gang's starting crew if their owner is, and do not count against crew or Reinforcement limits. When their owner activates, any Pets outside Leash range become Panicked. When the owner goes Out of Action or is removed, their Pets are also removed. Pets cannot control objectives, though they do prevent enemy Fighters from controlling them.
Prospect
When a Prospect is taken Out of Action, friendly models only need to make Nerve checks if they themselves are also Prospects. If during the Post-cycle Sequence a Prospect reaches at least 13 XP, they are promoted to a Ganger and gain the Specialist Subtype, increasing their Credit Cost by +15 credits.
Specialist
When added to the Gang Roster, must select one specialization, which grants a free skill (см. таблицу ниже).
Support
Can always be included in a gang's starting crew, does not count against the crew limit, and can never be targeted with a ranged attack unless they are the closest model.
Wyrd
May use Wyrd powers, and treats Wyrd Powers as a Secondary Skill Set.

Подтипы машинVehicle Subtypes

Определяются способом передвижения и влияют на взаимодействие с трудной и опасной местностью.

Hybrid
Uses a mix of methods for locomotion; it may choose which of its Locomotion Subtypes to use when activating.
Manoeuverable
May perform one additional 90° turn when performing the Charge or Dash action.
Skimmer
Ignores all difficult or dangerous terrain when moving. However, if it ends its activation on difficult or dangerous terrain it must make an Agility test; if failed, it suffers a Str –, AP –, L1 Hit that automatically Wounds and ignores all saves.
Tracked
Suffers no penalty for moving over difficult terrain.
Transport (X)
Has an enclosed cab designed to carry up to X Fighters, and must have at least one clearly visible access point. While embarked, Fighters are treated as not on the battlefield and may only perform the Interact action (to disembark within 1" of an access point). If the Vehicle is taken Out of Action, embarked Fighters are placed Suppressed within 1" of it; any that cannot be placed are automatically taken Out of Action. Brutes and Mounted Fighters cannot enter a Transport.
Walker
May make as many turns during its activation as it wishes. For every 1" of difficult terrain it moves through, it counts as having moved 2". Walkers are treated as Fighters for the purpose of being Engaged.
Wheeled
Able to reach high speeds but struggles over rough terrain. For every 1" of difficult terrain it moves through, it counts as having moved 2".

СостоянияConditions

В дополнение к статусу модель может иметь одно или несколько состояний одновременно.

Fearsome
If a model subject to the Fearsome Condition is selected as the target of close combat attacks, the attacking model must immediately make a Willpower check. If the check is failed, then until the current model's activation ends, set the attacking model's WS to 6+. Models subject to Fearsome are themselves not affected by it, unless the enemy model also has the Terrifying Condition.
Frenzy
An Active model subject to Frenzy must declare a Charge action if they begin their activation within their Movement characteristic +6" of an enemy model and are able to complete that Charge, moving directly towards the nearest enemy model. Подготовка к боюThe Pre-Battle Sequence

Семь шагов, выполняемых строго по порядку и в присутствии обоих игроков.

  1. Make a Challenge & Stake Territory. In Campaign play, challenges are handled as described by the Arbitrator, and an eligible Territory is nominated as the Stake. In Skirmish play, a challenge is issued and accepted when two players decide to play a battle.
  2. Determine Scenario. In Campaign play, the scenario is generated on the Scenario Generation Tables. In Skirmish play, players may simply choose the result on each table. In Campaign play the player who issued the challenge is the attacker; in Skirmish play both players roll a D6, and the highest chooses who is attacker and defender.
  3. Set Up the Battlefield.
  4. Choose Crews. The scenario's Crew table details the method of selection, and how many and what kind of models can be selected. Players can also spend credits to recruit Hired Guns; if both players wish to, the player with the higher Gang Rating must do so first.
  5. Announce Battlefield Effects. Players announce which Territories are applying Battlefield Effects to the upcoming battle. This step is ignored in Skirmish play.
  6. Gang Tactics. Each player may select one Gang Tactic they have access to; each may be used once during the battle.
  7. Deployment. Starting with the defender, players set their gangs up in the deployment zones defined by the scenario.
Underdog Bonus If one player's Gang Rating is at least 200 credits less than their opponent's, they may select an additional Gang Tactic for the battle for each full 200 credits difference in Gang Rating.

Структура раундаGame Structure

Каждый раунд состоит из трёх фаз, разрешаемых по шагам.

Priority Phase

  1. Ready Gangs — each player gives each of their models a Ready marker.
  2. Roll for Priority — each player rolls a D6; the highest has Priority for the round. On a tie, whoever did not have Priority last round has Priority. If the very first roll is a draw, re-roll until one player wins.

Action Phase

Activate Gangs — starting with the player who has Priority, players take turns choosing one of their Ready models to activate. When a model activates, it may perform two actions.

If one player has run out of models to activate, the other activates all of their remaining models in an order of their choosing. Once all models have activated, the Action phase ends.

End Phase

  2. Check For Victory
  3. Reinforcements

ДействияActions

Доступный набор действий зависит от текущего статуса модели. Одно и то же действие нельзя выполнить дважды за активацию, если правило прямо не разрешает.

Типы действийTypes of Action

  • Single actions — each counts as one of the model's actions. A first single action may be fully resolved before declaring the second.
  • Double actions — count as making two actions. If a model can only perform one action during its activation for any reason, it may not perform a double action.
  • Free actions — do not count as using one of the model's actions.
Changing Action Type Some effects may change an action's type; for example, when a model performs the Charge action, after they have moved they must perform a Fight action as a Free action.

After a model that started their activation Seriously Injured/Damaged completes their activation, the controlling player makes a Recovery test by rolling an Injury dice:

Перемещение моделейMoving Models

When a model moves, the action they are performing says how far they can move; unless stated otherwise, they do not have to move this full distance. A Fighter need not move in a straight line — they can zigzag around terrain as appropriate (a Fighter performing a Charge or Engage action should take the shortest route possible). A Vehicle must move in a straight line directly forwards, though unless stated otherwise it may perform as many turns as it wishes, pivoting around its Central Point.

Models may not move through other models, either friend or foe.

The 1" Rule Unless they are performing the Charge, Engage, Retreat or Crawl actions, a model may not move within 1" of an enemy model unless the enemy model is Seriously Injured/Damaged.
  • Charge / Engage: if a model moves within 1" of an enemy model, they must end their move in base contact with the model targeted by the action.
  • Retreat: a model may move within 1" of any enemy models, but must end the move at least 1" away from all of them.
  • Crawl: a Fighter may move and end within 1" of enemy models; however, if they are within 1" of an enemy Fighter who is not Seriously Injured when they make their Recovery test, they are automatically taken Out of Action.

Directly Towards & Directly Away From

Trace an imaginary straight line that crosses through the centre of each model — the moving model then moves the required distance towards or away from the other along this line. This cannot make a model move through a solid terrain feature or other model; should it come into contact with one, it stops and does not move any further.

Местность и препятствияTerrain & Obstacles

Agility Tests A model rolls a D6 — on a 4 or more they pass. Some abilities and skills modify this roll (for example, all Escher Fighters add +1 to Agility tests). Regardless of modifiers, a natural 1 is always a fail and a natural 6 is always a success.
Difficult terrain
When measuring how far a Fighter may move through difficult terrain, treat every 1" moved as 2". How a Vehicle interacts with difficult terrain depends on its Subtype.
Dangerous terrain
Treated as difficult terrain for measuring movement. Additionally, after moving through dangerous terrain a model must roll a D6; on a 1 they immediately suffer a Str –, AP –, L1 Hit that Wounds automatically and ignores all saves.
Obstacles
Fighters may cross obstacles as they move, but doing so reduces their Movement by a number of inches equal to the height of the obstacle. A Fighter may not end their movement on top of an obstacle.
Structures
Fighters may climb up and onto structures and between the various levels and platforms as they move, and may end their movement on any level if there is sufficient space for their base.
Ductways
Up to a maximum of 2" in length, placed across any 2"-wide wall or other otherwise-impassable terrain feature. They allow Fighters to traverse the feature using the Crawl Through Ductway action.
Doors
Fighters may open or close doors they are within 1" of at any point during their move, costing 2" of movement each time. A Fighter can open a door, move through it and then close it, providing they have sufficient movement. Locked doors cannot be opened by sacrificing movement, though they can still be destroyed; a Fighter within 1" of the access terminal may perform the Interact action to lock or unlock the door.
Fighter Movement on Vehicles Fighters treat Vehicles like terrain for the purposes of movement. Whenever a Vehicle moves, any Fighters on it (except those on designated transport areas) must immediately pass an Agility test or fall. If they fall, place them 1" away from the Vehicle in a direction determined by the Scatter dice after the Vehicle has finished moving. They only need to test once during each Vehicle's activation.

Карабканье, прыжки, паденияClimbing, Leaping & Falling

Climbing

Fighters can climb up or down any vertical surface to reach a higher level or platform during their movement. For every 1" a Fighter moves vertically by climbing, they count as having moved 2". A Fighter cannot end their activation mid-climb; they must have sufficient movement to reach a flat surface.

  • Stepping Up & Down: a Fighter may freely step up or down onto another level, provided it is no more than ½" higher or lower. If the difference is more than ½", they must climb or jump down.
  • Overhangs: a climbing Fighter can traverse an overhang as long as it protrudes no more than 1". Overhangs protruding more than 1" are impassable to a climbing Fighter.
  • Ladders & Stairs: no modifiers to a Fighter's movement.

Leaping Gaps

A Fighter can cross any gap of 2" or less with no need to roll, as long as they have sufficient movement to reach the other side and there is space for their base. To leap a gap larger than 2" they must make an Agility test; if failed, they fall from the point they were attempting to leap from. The only Vehicles that can cross gaps are Walkers, which only test for gaps larger than their base.

Falling Hazards

If a model is forcibly moved off a ledge, they must make an Agility test. If passed, they remain where they are and are not moved; if failed, they fall.

  • Railings: a Fighter applies a +1 modifier to the Agility test to avoid falling. A Vehicle gains no benefit from railings.
  • Jumping Down: a model that deliberately jumps down automatically falls, with no test needed. Make an Agility test to see how well they land; if passed, reduce the effect on the falling table by one level.

СтрельбаShooting

Четыре шага дальней атаки.

  1. Declare the Shot. Choose a ranged weapon profile the attacking model is equipped with and nominate an enemy model in Line of Sight to be the target.
  2. Measure Range. Measure the distance between the attacker and the target. If the target is out of range, the attack automatically misses.
  3. Make the Hit Roll. A Hit roll is a Ballistic Skill test made against the BS of the model making the shot.
  4. Resolve Hits. If the BS test is passed, a Hit is scored and each Hit is resolved (см. Разрешение попаданий).
Модификаторы и особые случаи
  • Target is Engaged: if the shot hits, all Hits must be randomly allocated between all models involved in the combat.
  • Natural 1: the shot misses, regardless of modifiers.
  • Natural 6: the shot hits, regardless of modifiers.

Hit Models Become Suppressed

When a model is Hit by a ranged attack they become Suppressed, unless they are also Engaged or Seriously Injured/Damaged.

Falling from Heights: if a model becomes Suppressed or Seriously Injured/Damaged as a result of being Hit by a ranged attack while within ½" of a ledge, they must immediately check to see if they fall. If they are within ½" of two ledges they fall off the closer one; if equidistant, the controlling player chooses.

Боеприпасы и Rapid FireRunning Out of Ammo

When a weapon is Out of Ammo, it cannot be used again until a Reload action is taken. Many weapons have multiple profiles representing different kinds of ammo; when a weapon goes Out of Ammo, only the profile used for the attack goes Out of Ammo. When the Reload action is performed, only one of a weapon's profiles (of the controlling player's choice) has the Out of Ammo Condition removed.

Rapid Fire (X) When firing a weapon with this trait, you may also roll a number of Firepower dice up to X. If the attack hits, resolve a number of hits equal to the number of bullet holes across all the Firepower dice.

Regardless of whether the shots Hit or not, if any ammo symbols are rolled the weapon has run out of ammo. If multiple ammo symbols are rolled then the weapon jams and cannot be used again this battle. A weapon with the Ammo (X+) trait may be considered to have rolled one additional ammo symbol.

Weapons with both the Rapid Fire (X) and Light traits can only fire single shots when used in close combat — the Firepower dice is never rolled in this manner.

Blast и TemplateBlast & Template Weapons

  • Hitting Engaged Models: if a weapon with either the Template or Blast (3"/5") trait hits a model that is Engaged, then every model (friend and foe) that model is Engaged with is automatically Hit.
  • Hitting Models on Different Levels: only models on the same level as the targeted model may be Hit. If a Blast marker scatters off a ledge, it remains on the same level and explodes in midair.

2. СпасбросокSave Roll

Roll a D6 and apply the weapon's armour-piercing characteristic as a modifier. If the result is equal to or greater than the model's Save characteristic, the Hit does no damage.

  • Only one Save roll may be made for each Hit that successfully Wounds, regardless of how many different types of Save a model may have.
  • Some weapon traits disallow Save rolls entirely (for example, Gas). In such cases, regardless of any modifiers, no Save roll can be made.
  • If a model has been successfully Wounded by several attacks at the same time, the Hit model's opposing player chooses the order in which the attacks are resolved.
  • Cover (ranged attacks only): +1 if the target is within the weapon's Short Range, +2 if within its Long Range. For a Blast weapon that has scattered, check cover from the direction of the centre of the Blast marker.
  • Invulnerable saves are not affected by modifiers from any source (for example, an attack's AP and Cover).
  • A natural 1 or 2 when making a Save roll is always an automatic failure, regardless of any modifiers.

If a successful Wound roll is not saved or mitigated, the target loses a Wound. If this results in the model's Wounds characteristic dropping to zero (or if it is already zero), the target's opposing player rolls a number of Injury dice equal to the weapon's Lethality characteristic, then selects one of the Injury dice and applies the result:

  • Serious Injury — the model becomes Seriously Injured (Fighter) or Seriously Damaged (Vehicle). If the model is already Seriously Injured/Damaged, this result has no effect.
  • Out of Action — the model is immediately removed from play and takes no further part in this battle. Roll on the Lasting Injury or Lasting Damage table.

A model's Wounds characteristic may never drop below zero.

Фаза окончанияThe End Phase

1. Bottle Checks

Starting with the player with Priority, if a gang has had at least one model taken Out of Action this round, they must make a Leadership check using their gang's highest Leadership characteristic on the battlefield. If this check is failed, one model of the controlling player's choice flees the battlefield. If the check result is a natural 12, two models must be selected instead. Models that are Engaged can only be selected if every friendly model is Engaged. Any Seriously Injured/Damaged models removed in this manner must check to see if they succumb.

Hold Your Nerve: a gang only needs to check if they had a model taken Out of Action this round. A model lost in a previous round does not trigger a check.

Voluntarily Fleeing the Battlefield Once at least a third (rounded up) of a gang's starting crew are taken Out of Action or have fled, that gang's controlling player can choose to flee the battlefield at the start of the Check For Victory step. All of the gang's models are removed and they automatically lose the scenario.

2. Check For Victory

Controlling Objectives: in order for a gang to control an objective, they must have at least one friendly Fighter without the Pet Subtype within 3" of the centre of the objective, and no enemy models (including Pets) within 3" of the centre. Disregard Seriously Injured/Damaged models when determining control.

For the purposes of gaining XP, only one Fighter (of the controlling player's choice) is considered to be controlling each objective. Neither Pets nor Vehicles can control objectives, though they do prevent enemy Fighters from controlling them.

After control of objectives has been determined, check to see if the Ending the Battle conditions for the scenario have been met.

3. Reinforcements

If either gang has Reinforcements arriving on the battlefield this round, players take turns deploying them.

Силы виардовWyrd Powers

Виарды активируются как обычные модели, но дополнительно могут проявлять или подавлять псионические силы.

Manifesting a Wyrd power may be either a Single, Double or Free action, depending upon the complexity of the power; this is always shown in brackets after the name of the power. When a Wyrd is activated, they may choose to attempt to manifest one or more Wyrd powers that they know.

Действия, доступные всем виардам

  • Maintain Control (Single): the Wyrd makes a Willpower check with a +3 modifier — if passed, a Continuous effect remains in effect until the end of their next activation; if failed, the power ends immediately.
  • Maintain Control (Free): performed at the start of their activation with no modifiers to the Willpower check; the Wyrd may not attempt to manifest another power this activation.
  • Concentrate (Single): applies a +1 modifier to their Willpower characteristic when manifesting any Wyrd powers during this activation.

Проявление и подавлениеManifesting & Disrupting

To manifest a Wyrd power, a Wyrd must make a Willpower check; if passed, the power is successfully manifested.

Once a Wyrd has declared they are going to attempt to manifest a power, before the Willpower check is made, any opposing Active or Suppressed Wyrds within 18" that have Line of Sight and are not Injured may attempt to disrupt the power (only one Wyrd may attempt to disrupt each power). The disrupting Wyrd makes a Willpower check; if successful, the manifesting Wyrd applies a −2 modifier to their Willpower.

  • Disrupt, natural 2: the power automatically fails to manifest; the disrupting Wyrd suffers Perils of the Warp.
  • Disrupt, natural 12: the attempt to disrupt automatically fails; the disrupting Wyrd suffers Perils of the Warp.
  • Manifest, natural 2: the power is always manifested regardless of modifiers. Resolve it fully, then roll on Perils of the Warp.
  • Manifest, natural 12: the power is never manifested regardless of modifiers. Immediately roll on Perils of the Warp.

Подготовка поля бояBattlefield Set-Up

АндхайвUnderhive

For underhive battles, between a 3'×3' and a 4'×4' battlefield is ideal, although much larger or smaller battlefields are also possible. Starting with the defender, players alternate placing terrain features upon the battlefield. Once one player declines to place any terrain, the other player may place one more piece. Then, starting with the defender again, players alternate placing obstacles and other small terrain features.

Tunnel Warfare If both players agree, the claustrophobic confines of tunnel battles can be recreated by stating that the ceiling is at the height of one wall section, meaning walls cannot be climbed over, and stairs and ladders cannot be used.

После бояThe Post-Battle Sequence

Пять шагов, выполняемых по порядку в присутствии обоих игроков.

  1. Wrap Up
  2. Reassign Territory
  3. Receive Rewards
  4. Clean House
  5. Report Results

1. Wrap Up

Succumbing to Injuries / Irreparably Damaged: if a model is Seriously Injured/Damaged when the battle ends, or fled the battlefield while Seriously Injured/Damaged, roll a D6. On a 3 or more they survive without any further complications; on a 1 or 2 they are treated as having gone Out of Action and a Lasting Injury/Damage roll is made for them.

Escape Table — для моделей с результатом Captured (61–62)
D6Result
1Executed: The model dies or is destroyed attempting to escape and is deleted from the Gang Roster.

5–6Daring Escape: The model escapes their captors and goes into Recovery.

2. Reassign Territory

The player that won the battle takes control of the Territory Staked on the battle. In the case of a draw while fighting for an uncontrolled Territory, it is not claimed by either player. In the case of a draw when a controlled Territory is at Stake, it does not change hands.

3. Receive Rewards

Each campaign details the rewards gangs receive based on their performance. Any equipment gained is added to the gang's Stash. If a gang both gains and loses Reputation, gains are applied before losses (a gang's Reputation may never drop below 1).

Опыт — Gaining Experience

  • 1 XP if they took part in a battle.
  • 1 XP if their action directly causes an enemy model to become Seriously Injured/Damaged.
  • 2 XP if their action directly causes an enemy model to go Out of Action — plus an extra 1 XP if that model is a Leader, Champion, Brute or Vehicle.
  • 1 XP if they provide Assistance to another Fighter making a Recovery test who successfully recovers.
  • 1 XP if they control an objective during the End phase of a round (once per battle per Fighter).

Models can only gain XP for Seriously Injuring or taking an enemy model Out of Action once per enemy model per activation — if a model causes an enemy to become Seriously Injured and go Out of Action, they only gain the 2 XP.

4. Clean House

Dead Fighters and Destroyed Vehicles are deleted from the Gang Roster. If their gang had at least one model on the battlefield at the end of the battle, all of a dead Fighter's equipment is added to the gang's Stash; otherwise it is lost. The controlling player can also choose to retire models — usually because Lasting Injuries have rendered them unfit for battle.

5. Report Results

The report should contain: which gangs fought, the outcome of the battle, which Territory the victor gained control of (if any) and how many enemies each gang took Out of Action.

Создание бандыGang Creation

Стандартный бюджет — 1000 кредитов, который нельзя превысить при создании банды.

Any unspent credits are added to the gang's Stash if the gang has been founded for Campaign play. If a gang has been founded for Skirmish play, any unspent credits are simply lost. The Arbitrator may increase or decrease the budget.

Состав бандыGang Composition

  • There must be one model with the Leader Subtype.
  • The total number of models with the Champion, Brute or Hanger-on Subtype must always be less than or equal to the number of models without any of those Subtypes.

Should the number of Champions/Brutes/Hangers-on exceed the rest during a campaign, the controlling player must either retire some of them or recruit fresh models without those Subtypes during the Post-cycle Sequence.

Designer's Note: Pets Pets are treated as wargear and are not counted when working out if a gang has too many models with the Champion, Brute or Hanger-on Subtype. Many players still find it handy to include Pets on the Gang Roster to track their XP, Lasting Injuries and Post-cycle Actions.

Оружие и снаряжениеWeapons & Wargear

  • A Fighter can be equipped with a maximum of three weapons purchased from those listed in their entry.
  • Grenades count as wargear, not weapons, and so do not count towards this maximum.
  • Weapons marked with an asterisk (*) take up the space of two weapons.
  • Any model may purchase wargear listed in the relevant equipment list.
  • Each weapon may have at most one weapon accessory added to it.
  • A Vehicle's entry will state if there is a maximum number of weapons it may be equipped with.

Equipment Sets

All models can have multiple Model Cards, each representing a different set of equipment. There is no additional cost for having multiple equipment sets — each individual weapon or item of wargear is only purchased and counted once. Only one of a model's cards can be used for a battle. If a battle uses random models, all of that model's cards are shuffled together and one is drawn at random before any cards are drawn for the crew.

Смерть лидераDeath of a Leader

If a gang's Leader is killed, the new Leader is the Fighter with the highest Leadership characteristic, selected from among those Fighters that have, in order of priority:

  1. The Champion Subtype, but not the Loner Subtype.
  2. Both the Champion and Loner Subtypes.
  3. Neither the Beast, Brute, Pet nor Support Subtype.

Vehicles and Hangers-on can never become a gang's Leader; if the only models left in a gang are Vehicles and Hangers-on, the gang is dissolved. Ties are broken by total XP, then by the controlling player's choice. A promoted Fighter gains the Leader Subtype and loses the Loner, Champion, Ganger and Prospect Subtypes; their existing characteristics and special rules do not change, and they keep equipment not allowed by their new role, though they may no longer purchase additional gear granted only to their original role.

Структура кампанииUsing the Campaign

Кампания — это серия боёв за контроль над территориями, разделённая на три фазы.

  1. Occupation Phase (3 Cycles)
  2. Downtime (1 Cycle)
  3. Takeover Phase (3 Cycles)

A Necromunda campaign is typically divided into six campaign Cycles, separated by a single Cycle of Downtime after the first three, for a total of seven Cycles. For ease, each Cycle is usually a week.

АрбитрThe Arbitrator

  • Organizing the players and making a list of all the gangs involved in the campaign.
  • Creating the Territory table and Scenario tables.
  • Keeping the players informed as to the progression of the campaign and tracking control of Territories.
  • Deciding when each phase of the campaign begins and ends, as well as when the overall campaign starts and finishes.

ТерриторииTerritories

Каждая территория даёт бандe-владельцу бонус (Boon) и влияет на бой, в котором она является ставкой (Battlefield Effect).

Boons come in five kinds: Income (credits added to the Stash during the Post-cycle Sequence), Recruit (free or discounted models — recruits stay with the gang even if the Territory is later lost), Equipment (added to the Stash, and not lost with the Territory), Reputation (lost with the Territory), and Special.

Agility

Catfall (Fighter or Walker)
When this model falls or jumps down from a ledge, they count the vertical distance as being one level less than normal. In addition, if they are not Seriously Injured/Damaged or taken Out of Action by a fall, make an Agility test — if passed, they do not become Suppressed.
Clamber (Fighter)
When this Fighter climbs, the distance they move is not halved; they always count as climbing up or down a ladder.
Dodge (Fighter or Vehicle)
If this model suffers a Wound from a shooting or close combat attack (including from the Unstable trait), roll a D6. On a 6, the attack is dodged and has no further effect. Against a Blast marker or flame Template, a 6 instead allows the model to move up to 2" before seeing if they are Hit.
Mighty Leap (Fighter or Walker)
When measuring the distance of a gap the model wishes to leap across, ignore the first 2". This means they may leap gaps of 4" or less without making an Agility test.
Spring Up (Fighter)
If this Fighter is Suppressed when activated, make an Agility test. If passed, the Fighter is no longer Suppressed and may perform two actions as normal.
Sprint (Fighter or Walker)
While Active, this model may perform the Sprint (Double) action: move a distance equal to their Movement plus twice their Initiative in inches.

Brawn

Bull Charge (Fighter or Vehicle)
When the model makes close combat attacks as part of a Charge action, any close combat weapons they use gain the Knockback (6+) trait and are resolved at +1 Strength.
Bulging Biceps (Fighter)
When performing a Braced Shot action, the Fighter may move a distance equal to their Initiative in inches either before or after resolving the attack. When attacking with a Heavy weapon in close combat, they may declare a Secondary weapon as long as it is not also Heavy.
Fearsome (Fighter or Vehicle)
This model is subject to the Fearsome Condition.
Iron Jaw (Fighter or Vehicle)
This model's Toughness is treated as being 2 higher than normal when resolving Hits from a close combat attack with an AP characteristic of '–'.
Nerves of Steel (Fighter or Vehicle)
When the model is Hit by a ranged attack, they may make a Cool check. If passed, they are not Suppressed by the attack.
Unstoppable (Fighter or Vehicle)
Each time this model activates, before performing any actions, make a Willpower check. If passed, they recover 1 Wound lost earlier in the battle.

Свойства оружияWeapon Traits

Алфавитный список наиболее часто встречающихся трейтов.

Additional Attacks (X)
The weapon can be used to make X attacks during close combat in addition to all normal attacks. This only applies during the model's activation and only if the weapon is not designated as either the model's Primary or Secondary weapon.
Ammo (X+)
After the weapon has fired, roll a D6. If the result is X or more, there is ammo left. Otherwise, it gains the Out of Ammo Condition automatically, even if no Firepower dice are rolled. If an Out of Ammo result is also rolled on the Firepower dice, the weapon jams and cannot be used again for the duration of the battle.
Arc (X)
The weapon has limited fields of fire, as denoted by X. A model can only target models within that field of fire.
Assault
After the bearer has performed a Dash action, they may perform a Shoot action with this weapon as a Free action.
Auxiliary
Can only be attached to another weapon and not taken on its own. It does not use one of the model's three weapon slots.
Backstab
The weapon gains +1 Strength if the target of the attack is Engaged with more than one model.
Blast (3"/5")
Place either the 3" or 5" Blast marker centred on the targeted model. If the Hit roll succeeds, the template stays where it is. If it misses, scatter it D6" in a random direction determined by the Scatter dice, stopping if it hits impassable terrain or would go off the battlefield. If a Hit symbol and a 1 are rolled on the Scatter dice, the shot misfired — centre the marker on the firing model. Any model with their base at least partially under the marker suffers one automatic Hit. If a weapon has both Blast and Rapid Fire (X), resolve the first Blast marker as usual and scatter additional markers from its final location.
Blaze (X+)
If the Wound roll is at least X, the target suffers an additional Hit with the same profile as the first, even if the first fails to Wound. This additional Hit rolls to Wound separately and cannot cause further additional Hits. A weapon may never benefit from more than one instance of this trait.
Breaching (X+)
If the attack successfully Wounds and the Wound roll is at least X, Armour saves cannot be made against the attack.
Combi
When fired as part of a Shoot or Aimed Shot action, the controller chooses which profile to resolve the attack with before rolling. They can attack with both profiles targeting the same model, applying a −1 modifier to the Hit roll for each profile; these attacks are resolved simultaneously.
Concussive (X+)
If the attack successfully Wounds and the Wound roll is at least X, the target reduces their Initiative by 1 until the end of their next activation.
Cursed
A model Hit by a Cursed weapon must pass a Willpower check or become subject to the Insanity Condition. If the Hit roll is a natural 1, the bearer must pass a Willpower check or become subject to Insanity.
Damage (X)
A Wounded model loses X Wounds rather than the usual one. If this results in Injury dice being rolled, the number rolled equals the Lethality characteristic of the weapon, regardless of the number of Wounds lost.
Drag
If a model is Hit but not taken Out of Action, the attacker can attempt to drag the target closer. Roll a D6; if the score is equal to or higher than the target's Strength, the target is dragged D3" directly towards the attacker, stopping if they hit terrain. This movement can cause models to fall. Any models that end within 1" of an enemy are moved the shortest distance possible to be Engaged.
Flash
No Wound roll is made. Instead, the target must make an Initiative check; if failed, they become subject to the Blind Condition. A Blind model loses their Ready marker (or does not gain one at the start of the following round). Until the next time the model is activated, any close combat attacks they make only Hit on a natural 6.
Gas
Armour saves cannot be taken against attacks with this trait. Models equipped with a respirator gain a 5+ Invulnerable save against Wounds caused by it.
Graviton Pulse
Instead of rolling to Wound, any model caught in the Blast area must make a Strength check. If failed, they are Wounded with no Armour saves allowed. Leave the Blast marker in place; for the remainder of the round, any model moving through the area treats it as difficult terrain. At the start of each End phase, roll a D6 for each such marker — on a 5+ it remains for another round, otherwise it is removed.
Heavy
A ranged weapon with this trait can only be fired using the Braced Shot action. A close combat weapon with this trait may not be selected as a Secondary weapon, and a Secondary weapon may not be used alongside it.
Independent
Cannot be fired by the bearer. Instead, it is fired in addition to other weapons whenever the bearer performs a ranged attack, and may target a different model. All hits are resolved with a BS of 4+, which cannot be modified by the bearer's skills, equipment or special rules.
Knockback (X+)
If the weapon hits a Fighter and the Hit roll is at least X, the Fighter is moved 1" directly away from the attacker. If this would cause them to fall off a ledge, they test for falling. This can also cause models to no longer be Engaged. With a Blast weapon, roll a D6 for each Fighter Hit after the marker's final position is determined; on a result of at least X they are moved 1" directly away from the centre of the marker.
Lance
While equipped by a Fighter with the Mounted Subtype, the weapon increases its Strength by 1 for any attacks made as part of a Charge action.
Lance-bomb
The first successful Hit scored each battle is resolved using the Primed profile; all subsequent hits use the Spent profile.
Light
May be used as either a Primary or Secondary weapon in close combat, though only one attack may be made with it, regardless of the model's Attacks characteristic, skills or weapon traits. If a weapon has both Template and Light, then in close combat one opposing enemy model suffers one automatic Hit — do not place the template.
Limited
If the weapon runs out of ammo, it cannot be reloaded and may not be used again for the remainder of the battle.
Melee
The weapon is used to make attacks while Engaged.
Paired (X)
Can never be selected as a Secondary weapon, and a Secondary weapon may not be used alongside it. When selected as a Primary weapon, the bearer increases their Attacks characteristic by X.
Parry
If a model uses this weapon as either their Primary or Secondary weapon when resolving a combat, they increase their Save characteristic by 1. Using more than one weapon with this trait confers no additional benefit.
Power Pack
Does not count towards the maximum number of weapons a Fighter can carry. However, a Fighter cannot have more than two weapons with this trait.
Rad-phage
When a Fighter suffers an unsaved Wound from this weapon, they gain the Rad Poisoned Condition, reducing their Toughness by 1 (to a minimum of 1).
Ram
Can only be used during an activation when the bearer has performed the Charge action.
Rapid Fire (X)
Follows the rules for Rapid Fire (см. Стрельба).
Reckless
Before making a shooting attack, randomly determine the target from all models (friend or foe) within the model's Line of Sight, within 6" of the intended target and within range. Attacks with a Reckless close combat weapon are randomly distributed between any models (friend or foe) Engaged with this model.
Reliable
Ignores the first Out of Ammo symbol rolled each round on the Firepower dice.
Rending (X+)
If the natural Wound roll is at least X, increase the AP characteristic of the weapon for this attack by 1 (AP −1 would become AP −2).
Scarce (X+)
When the Reload action is performed, instead of automatically having more ammo the player must roll a D6. If the result is at least X the weapon is successfully reloaded; otherwise it is still Out of Ammo, though further attempts can be made in later activations.
Shield
If a model is equipped with at least one weapon with this trait, they increase their Save characteristic by 1 against shooting attacks. Multiple such weapons confer no further benefit.
Shock (X+)
If the attack Hits and the Hit roll is at least X, it automatically Wounds and is considered to have rolled a natural 6 on the Wound roll for the purposes of any other traits.
Shred (X+)
If the weapon Wounds a model and the Wound roll is at least X, increase the Lethality characteristic of the weapon for that attack by 1.
Single Shot
Can only be fired once per battle, at which point it automatically goes Out of Ammo and cannot be reloaded by any means.
Smoke
Does not have to target an enemy model; it may instead target a point on the battlefield. Leave the Blast marker in place — a column of smoke of infinite height rises up and blocks Line of Sight (stopping at the edge of a terrain feature it hits). During the Reinforcement step of the End phase, roll a D6 for each such marker; on a 5+ it remains, otherwise it is removed. A model Hit by a smoke grenade is never Wounded and is not Suppressed.
Template
Place the teardrop-shaped template so that the narrow end is in base contact with the firing model and the targeted model is under the template. Any model with any part of their base under the template is automatically Hit — no Hit roll is needed. With Template + Light in close combat, one opposing enemy model suffers one automatic Hit instead. With Template + Rapid Fire (X), the model closest to the firing model is Hit a number of times determined by the Firepower dice; all other models are Hit just once.
Toxin (X+)
Against a Fighter, instead of comparing Strength to Toughness, the target is Wounded on a result of X+. Against a Vehicle, the target is only Wounded on a natural 6.
Twin-linked
When making a ranged attack, the Firepower dice may be re-rolled (all of the Firepower dice must be re-rolled).
Unstable
If the Hit roll is a natural 1, the model using the weapon suffers an automatic Hit using this weapon's profile and no other models are Hit. If the weapon does not need to roll to Hit (for example, it has the Template trait), roll a D6 before resolving the attack as described.
Unwieldy
When a model attacks with this weapon in close combat, their Initiative is set to 1 before applying any modifiers.
Web
Armour saves cannot be taken, though Invulnerable saves may still be taken. If a Fighter is Wounded by a Web weapon, then instead of losing a Wound they gain the Webbed Condition.
